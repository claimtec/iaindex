## 📋 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Is AIIndex open source?**
A: The protocol is open source (MIT). The verification infrastructure is proprietary SaaS. Think "open core" model like Red Hat or Elastic.

**Q: How is this different from robots.txt?**
A: robots.txt is advisory—it suggests what crawlers shouldn't access. AIIndex is enforced—it returns HTTP 403 if policy is violated. Plus it differentiates training vs. retrieval and provides cryptographic receipts.

**Q: What if AI companies ignore the protocol?**
A: That's why we have signed denial receipts. If a client violates policy, we have cryptographic proof—admissible in court. Plus our bot reputation system auto-blocks bad actors after 5 violations.

**Q: Is this GDPR compliant?**
A: Yes, by design. AIIndex provides the documented consent (Art 6) and records of processing (Art 30) that GDPR requires. No raw IPs are stored—everything is hashed or anonymized.

**Q: Can publishers monetize their data?**
A: That's on the roadmap. Right now, AIIndex is about control and transparency. In the future, we'll enable licensing frameworks where publishers can set prices for training data access.

**Q: Why would AI companies use this?**
A: Legal clarity. Right now, AI companies face lawsuits and uncertainty. AIIndex provides a standardized way to request consent, document it, and prove compliance. It's like HTTPS—initially optional, eventually mandatory.
